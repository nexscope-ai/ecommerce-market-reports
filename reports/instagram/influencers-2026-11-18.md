## What you can learn

The report selects 100 individual creators and celebrities from 188 verified candidates. 74 accounts have an available sampled engagement rate.

## How to interpret this report

Ranking basis: **Follower count among verified individual accounts**.

Brands, media, teams and groups are excluded. This is a curated candidate set, not an exhaustive census; follower counts do not establish campaign fit.
